Use the papercraft agent skill (github.com/xikhar/papercraft) to make a new piece of art: an original pop-up paper diorama, built in three.js, rendered headless to video. Design your own world, palette and pieces; don't reuse the skill's countryside example.

The world: a small fishing cove at golden hour. A teal sea with foam lines on the left, a sandy beach, a boardwalk winding through the middle. A striped red and cream lighthouse on a rocky point, four fishing huts with lit windows and smoking chimneys, lanterns along the boardwalk, a jetty out over the water with a moored sailboat, more sailboats and buoys offshore, beached rowboats. Palms as slotted crosses, dune grass, sea pinks, rocks, drying nets, fish crates, barrels, starfish, crabs and gulls. A small paper kid in a yellow raincoat and sou'wester carrying a lantern stands on the boardwalk.

Then make a video: a slow camera pan across the whole village, starting over the jetty and the sea, past the lighthouse, over the huts at the back and down to the kid. Everything lies flat on the page until the camera reaches it, then folds up on its hinge with a little overshoot, neighbours staggered. 1280 × 720, 24 fps, about 14 seconds.
