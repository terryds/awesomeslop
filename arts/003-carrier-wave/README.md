# carrier wave

Three stones dropped in a pond, rendered by a terminal.

## what you're looking at

Three points drift around the screen, each sending out rings. Where two crests meet they add up; where a crest meets a trough they cancel. That is interference, the same thing that makes noise-cancelling headphones work.

The picture has only ten brightness levels, because it has only ten characters: ` .:-=+*#%@`. The pink marks the rare places where all three waves agree.

## things worth stealing from the prompt

- **Giving the exact ramp.** Left alone, agents pick long 70-character ramps that look like mush at this size. Ten characters keep the bands crisp.
- **"Correct for that so the ripples are round."** Character cells are not square. Naming the trap up front saves a round of "why is it squashed?"
- **Saying what the focus is.** "These pink crests are the focus of the piece" tells the agent which effect must survive if something has to give.

## try changing

- one source instead of three (clean rings, no interference)
- a ramp made of box-drawing characters or a single word
- tie one of the sources to the pointer
