Make a single self-contained HTML file: a 60-second, looping, '90s Indonesian TV commercial for a mosquito coil (obat nyamuk bakar) that never existed, called "NYENYAK". Every frame is drawn live with JavaScript on a 2D canvas, and it should look like it was taped off the air and played back on an old tube TV. No libraries, no images, fonts or audio files: everything, including the jingle, is made in code. All on-screen text is in Indonesian. Do not use any real brand, slogan or jingle.

THE LOOK
- Draw the cartoon small, at 640 × 480: flat colours, dark marker outlines that wobble a little (re-roll the wobble 8 times a second), fat block lettering with a hard drop shadow, comic bursts, spinning sunburst backgrounds and '80s/'90s confetti shapes (squiggles, triangles, rings, plus signs).
- Then ruin it like a tape. Redraw the frame in thin horizontal strips, each nudged sideways a hair, with one band of tracking noise crawling up the picture, torn rows along the very bottom edge, a hard jolt of tearing at every cut, lifted purplish blacks, a little washed-out white, and grain.
- Then show it on a tube. Split the picture into a red copy and a cyan copy and lay them back down slightly apart, add a faint ghost to the right, scanlines, a soft vignette, a glare in the top left, a slight mains flicker, and clip it all to a 4:3 screen with rounded corners and gently bulging edges on black.
- Cut between some shots with a hard cut and between others with a bloom to white.
- A "PLAY ▶" on-screen display for the first seconds, and a made-up station logo ("TV SENJA", a setting sun) in the corner during the ad.

5. 27–31 s. Bloom to white, then the product on a yellow sunburst: a red and yellow box, "NYENYAK, obat nyamuk bakar, isi 10 keping, 8 JAM*". "TENANG, PAK!" then "ADA NYENYAK!" The mascot pops up beside it: a green coil with a face, white gloves and a thumbs up. "TING!"
6. 31–39 s. Back in the bedroom. A match lights the coil on the side table ("CESSS"), the tip glows orange, and the smoke climbs as a chain of white puffs that closes into a fist. It punches the three mosquitoes one by one ("DUAK!", "BUK!", "PLETAK!"); each spins away with crosses for eyes. The father smiles. "ASAPNYA GALAK!" and, small, "tahan 8 jam*".
7. 39–45 s. Outside, night: a big moon, rooftops, a TV aerial, a coconut palm. The three mosquitoes fly off towards the moon with plasters on their heads and bundles on sticks, sweating. "NYAMUK MINGGAT!" and, small, "(ke rumah tetangga)".
8. 45–53 s. Sing-along: "AYO NYANYI!" over a blue panel with the two lines "NYAMUK MINGGAT, / TIDUR NYENYAK!". Each syllable turns yellow and pops on its beat, with a glowing ember bouncing along as the ball. The mascot dances on the corner of the panel. Sing it through twice.
9. 53–57 s. Pack shot: the father asleep and smiling with z's floating up, the box, the coil smoking gently. "NYAMUK MINGGAT, TIDUR NYENYAK." Fine print along the bottom: "*nyamuk dalam iklan ini adalah aktor profesional. tidak ada bapak yang digigit."
10. 57–60 s. Station bumper, teal with stars: "DAN KINI... KEMBALI KE SINETRON!" with the mascot waving. Loop.
