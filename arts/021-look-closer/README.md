# look closer

Five paintings, each one in the middle of the last, in a loop. A room at night has a painting on its wall; the painting is a street, and through the bakery window there is the bakery; on the bakery's wall is a postcard of a frozen lake; through a hole in the ice there is a reef; and sunk in the sand is a frame holding the room again.

**Scroll, drag or use the arrow keys** to walk in or back out. Space pauses. Left alone it drifts inward, about one painting every thirteen seconds.

## how it was made

- **The hole.** Every painting has a transparent square in its middle, a third of its width. The page draws the paintings from the innermost outward, each a third of the size of the one around it, so each one shows through the hole in the one outside it. Zooming in by three times lands exactly on the next painting, and after five it is back at the first.
- **The frames are part of the scene.** Something in each painting holds the hole: a gilded frame, a shop window, a postcard with a pin, a thick rim of ice, a frame half-buried in sand. They are drawn on top, after the hole is cut.
- **Painted, not traced.** Each scene is first drawn as flat shapes, then repainted with about 90,000 short strokes. Each stroke takes its colour from the shape underneath, nudged a little, and its angle from a flow field for that scene: vertical in the wallpaper, horizontal on the ice, swaying underwater. A faint canvas weave and a vignette go on top.
- **No assets.** Nothing is loaded; all five paintings are drawn when the page opens, which takes a second or two.
