# make a wish

A birthday card. A cake draws itself in pastel in a dark room. Click to light the candles and a music box plays; make a wish; click again to blow them out.

**To send one:** open "make one for someone" in the bottom left corner, type a name and an age, and press "share it". It copies the link, ready to paste into a message. "share this card" does the same for the card you are looking at. Or write it by hand: `?name=Ada&age=7`. Up to ten, you get that many candles. After that you get number candles.

**Sound:** the tune starts with the click that lights the candles. M turns it off.

## what you're looking at

- **A drawing.** About 20,000 strokes of soft pastel, oil pastel, coloured pencil and chalk on dark plum paper, laid down in the order a person would. The grain of the paper is one fixed mask, so the same specks of bare paper show through every layer.
- **A second sheet that moves.** Flames, smoke, the snow outside, the steam off the tea and the little bulbs are redrawn twelve times a second with the same tools and through the same grain, so they wobble like hand-drawn animation and never look pasted on.
- **Light, added last.** The drawing is made as if the candles were lit. When they are out, the whole sheet is multiplied by a dim blue; when they are lit, a warm glow is added around each flame.
- **The name is chalk.** Whatever you type is drawn into a hidden mask and the mask is filled with chalk strokes, written left to right. Any name in any script works.
- **The tune** is Happy Birthday on a music box, made from a few sine waves in the page. There are no audio files.

## things worth stealing from the prompt

- **Draw the lit version, then darken it.** One drawing, two moods, and the change costs one multiply.
- **Animate at 12 frames a second, in the same tools.** Moving parts that share the paper grain belong to the drawing.
- **Say what does not happen.** "No confetti, no fireworks" is what keeps a birthday piece quiet.
- **Make the text a mask, not markup.** It solves fonts, long names and safety at once.

## limits

- The link holds the name, so anyone who has the link can read it.
- Browsers only allow sound after a click, so the gallery preview is silent.
- The greeting is in English.
- Blowing is a click. It does not listen to the microphone.
